🔤 Unfamiliar terms explained simply
ILI — Integrated Life Index
Short for Integrated Life Index. It fuses all Eastern & Western data — Saju, NASA celestial data, rPPG biosignals, numerology — to express your current energy state as 0–100 points. The higher the score, the better your energy today!
rPPG — Remote Photoplethysmography
Short for remote PhotoPlethysmoGraphy. camera-read skin color changes— a technology analyzing these to measure heart rate, blood flow & stress. Measured from facial video alone, without touching — a contactless method. (CGO applies this academically studied technology fused with divination & AR)
Five Elements (五行) — Wood·Fire·Earth·Metal·Water
The core of Eastern philosophy. It classifies all things into Wood (木)·Fire (火)·Earth (土)·Metal (金)·Water (水) five energies. Your birth date sets your elements, used in compatibility, shopping, food recommendations and more.
BEI — Brain Endurance Index
Brain Endurance Index. While solving IQ questions, accuracy·reaction time·and inner resilienceare analyzed together — CGO's own index distinguishing guessing from real skill.
SVI — Skin Vitality Index
Skin Vitality Index. Analyzes how fast your skin surface recovers after press-and-release, analyzing it by camera to quantify recovery elasticity. Use your smartphone instead of a costly beauty device!
Inner resilience — stress recovery elasticity
Heart Rate Variability. Measures the variation between heartbeats. Higher inner resilience means faster stress recovery. It's measured by rPPG scan.
MUFE — Dynamic Multi-Factor Security
CGO's own security technology. Post-quantum standard cryptography (ML-KEM-768) · real-time chaos key · biometric verificationare bound in 9 layers, designed so the password DB is never stored off-device (zero-storage).
VSOP87 — Celestial orbit calculation theory
The high-precision planetary orbit theory used by NASA JPL. in microseconds (millionths of a second), it computes solar-system planet positions and applies them to divination in real time.
50 divination systems — a full set of Eastern & Western philosophy
Saju, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Qi Men Dun Jia, Nine Star Ki, I Ching, Feng Shui, numerology, Tarot, Western astrology, Kabbalah and more — 30 Eastern + 20 Western systems applied together. A balanced analysis that doesn't lean on any single method!
BAI — Bio Activity Index
Bio Activity Index. Computed by combining inner resilience·vitality tone·facial rhythm·and biorhythm, your current total bio-energy score.
Perpetual Calendar (萬歲曆) — the base calendar for Saju
It converts your birth year·month·day·hour into Heavenly Stems & Earthly Branches (60 Gapja), a traditional calendar. It's the starting point for face reading, Saju, compatibility, etc., auto-computed from your entered birth date/time.
CSI — Conflict-Offset Index
Conflict-Offset Index. When the 50 systems reach different conclusions, it reconciles the conflicts into an overall scoreand transparently flags 'disagreement' when they diverge — CGO's own reconciliation method.

Q. The camera bio-scan isn't working ▼

A. You need to allow browser camera permission.
📱 iOS Safari: Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow
🤖 Android Chrome: Address-bar lock → Permissions → Allow camera
💡 Installing as a PWA makes permissions easier!

Q. My ILI score is too low ▼

A. ILI reflects today's energy. On low days, it's best not to overexert.
To improve accuracy, ① enter your name, birth date & time correctly and ② complete all 4 bio-scans!

Q. I want to install the app on my home screen ▼

A. the bottom 📲 Install button, or:
📱 iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
🤖 Android: Chrome → Menu → Add to Home Screen

Q. How do I measure SVI skin elasticity? ▼

A. Go to All Features → Skin Elasticity Analysis (SVI), then
hold the camera close to your skin and, on the on-screen circle, press and release with your finger. It's more accurate under bright light!

A. No. All analyses are for infotainment & wellness reference, not a medical diagnosis. If you are worried about your health, be sure to consult a medical institution.

Q.Do I have to enter my info?▼

A. Yes. Saju, divination & ILI need your birth date/time to be computed. Before entering info, all features are locked . (Dashboard → Enter info)

Q.What is an rPPG bio-scan?▼

Q.My readings differ each time▼

A. Biosignals (heart rate, blood flow) vary with condition, lighting & posture — varying each time is normal. Try measuring several times under the same conditions.

Q.Is 'World's First' real?▼

A. It refers to CGO's own fusion structure, CGO-FULI's integrated technology. The key is the 'structure' binding these together, not the individual parts.

Q.Are IQ·BEI scores certified tests?▼

A. No. FULI's own scale (beta), not a Mensa or clinical IQ test. Please treat it as fun & self-check.

Q.Is my data safe? Where is it stored?▼

A. By default, measurement data is processed on-device. MUFE security aims for post-quantum cryptography and a zero-storage structure. (see the Privacy Policy for details)

A. Earn Stars via check-ins & activity, used for some features. My Page → Vault, where you can check them or top up at the Star Station.

Q.Should I trust the stocks, lucky numbers & fortunes?▼

A. All For reference & entertainment. Stocks especially are not investment advice — decisions & responsibility are your own.

Q.Where do I see the Detailed Analysis Report?▼
